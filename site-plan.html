Project Site Plan

Adelia Studio

This name represents the power and delicacy of women, but it is not only for women: skin care is for everybody and is a lifestyle.

Optional domain availability: adelia-studio.org

Purpose

The site provides simple steps and tips to start your skin care routine, and a list of products you can find in each supermarket.

Expected Scenarios

How can I start taking care of my skin?

What is the best product for my skin type?

Where can I find more help on my skin care path?

Color Scheme

We chose a natural palette: light purple for titles and buttons, pearl for the background of boxes, and cream for actions and to mark which part of the website you are in.

This palette creates a soothing and elegant look, and helps customers feel change and improve themselves, which is the purpose of skin care and of the company.

Typography

Merriweather for titles

Open Sans for paragraphs and notes
